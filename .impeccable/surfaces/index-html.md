# Surface brief: Sukkarah home page (index.html)

Scope: the single public home page. Visitor mode: Persuade (browse-only showcase for now; no ordering flow).
Audience: Arabic-first visitors in Qatar on phones, choosing sweets for a visit, a gathering, coffee, or a gift.
Action: see what Sukkarah makes ("what's inside the box"). Proof: the eight real kitchen photographs. Constraints: no prices, hours, location or contact invented; placeholders are labelled.
Memorable moment: the lid window opening into the box.
Unresolved: product names for the layered and wrapped trays, prices, contact details, real logo artwork.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one Sukkarah sweets box, lid first, then opened. It refuses the cream-and-burgundy patisserie page with a hero photo over a row of equal product cards.

OWN-WORLD: Deep pistachio-green printed board, brass-foil wordmark, rose paper seal band, rose tissue lining, white sticker labels with a rose border. Ruqʿah display for names, a clean Arabic sans for text. Square board corners, crease lines, no shadows except the lid edge.

STORY: The visitor sees the real tray through the lid window, reads the sticker of what's inside, opens the box, browses each compartment, and leaves knowing the name and what to bring for gifts and coffee.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed green lid. Rose seal band across the top holds wordmark and nav. Centre-right, a large die-cut window (about 45% width) shows the layered tray photo. Left, the lid sticker: headline, contents list, primary link "شوف ما في العلبة".

FORM: The sweets-shop box, position 3 of 7 on the ordered list. seed:d255c5263fbc7d6e. Signature: the window widens on scroll to open the box; motion is the lid lift, once.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
