# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No build step; must deploy to any static host (GitHub Pages, Netlify).

## Users

People in Qatar looking for Arabic sweets: for their own table, a family gathering, a guest visit with coffee, or a gift to bring someone. Mostly Arabic-speaking and browsing on a phone; some English-speaking residents.

## Product Purpose

Sukkarah (سكّرة) is a small Arabic sweets kitchen in Qatar. The site lets visitors see what Sukkarah makes and how it is served or gifted. For now the site is a browse-only showcase: success is a visitor who recognises the sweets, wants one, and remembers the name.

## Positioning

Trays and rounds from one kitchen, photographed as they are served at home on a real side table: kunafa, basbousa with almonds, a layered semolina-and-cream tray with a caramelised top and pistachio. The proof is the photographs, not claims.

## Operating Context

Sweets are served on trays with Arabic coffee, carried to visits, and given as wrapped gift trays. Photographs are taken at home: pink curtain, brass vase with tulips, marble side table, gold tray and coasters.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Browse only. No cart, checkout or ordering flow yet.
- Arabic is the primary language (RTL); English is a secondary toggle.
- Undecided and must not be invented: final product names for the layered tray and the wrapped tray, prices, sizes, ingredients and allergens, location, opening hours, phone/WhatsApp, Instagram handle.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: سكّرة, Latin transliteration SUKKARAH.
- Wordmark: the Arabic script "سكّرة" with a small diamond above the kāf, and "SUKKARAH" in spaced capitals beneath.
- The earlier burgundy-and-cream page is not binding; a new visual identity was requested.

## Evidence on Hand

Eight real photographs in `assets/photos/`: layered tray (2), basbousa with almonds (3), a layered-tray slice with Arabic coffee, a wrapped gift tray, a kunafa round. No testimonials, customer counts, press or prices exist; do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. The sweets lead; every section earns its place by showing them.
2. Arabic first, written for people who already know these sweets.
3. Never state a price, ingredient or fact the kitchen has not confirmed; mark placeholders plainly.
4. Ready to grow into ordering later without a redesign.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Correct RTL layout and Arabic typography; legible on small phones; WCAG AA contrast.
